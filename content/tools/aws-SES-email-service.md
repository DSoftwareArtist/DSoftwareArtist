# AWS Email Service

I've worked with AWS Simple Email Service (SES) for sending transactional and marketing emails in various applications. Integrating SES with Django, Vue.js, and other frameworks, I’ve set up email authentication using SPF, DKIM, and DMARC to improve deliverability and prevent spoofing. I’ve configured SMTP and API-based email sending, handling bounces and complaints through SNS for better monitoring. Additionally, I’ve optimized email templates, used dedicated IPs for higher sending reputation, and implemented AWS Lambda for automated email processing. My experience with SES has helped in creating reliable and scalable email solutions for different use cases.
