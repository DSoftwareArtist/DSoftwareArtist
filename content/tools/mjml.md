# MJML

I’ve used MJML to create responsive and well-structured email templates that ensure compatibility across different email clients. By leveraging MJML’s component-based syntax, I’ve streamlined email development, reducing inconsistencies and improving maintainability. I’ve also optimized email deliverability by integrating MJML templates with AWS SES, SendGrid, and Mailgun, ensuring high engagement and inbox placement.
